Why you can ask for better terms

    Before you accept

    • Ask about every fee added to the loan, including processing fees above 1% plus tax.
    • Don't accept insurance or other add-ons unless you understand and want them.
    • Ask whether there's a charge for repaying the loan early.
    • Ask for the total amount you'll repay before you accept the loan.

    Why we're saying don't borrow right now

    Your current loan payments and monthly expenses leave very little room for another loan. Taking one on now could make your existing commitments harder to manage.

    Your safe borrowing limit: ₹0

    What to do first

    1. Clear any overdue payments.
    2. Reduce your existing debt where you can.
    3. Keep your monthly expenses under control.
    4. Build a small emergency fund before you borrow again.
